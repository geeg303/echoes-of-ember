# World 3 Design — The Hollow Deep

**Status:** PROPOSED working name and structure.

## Identity and purpose

Subterranean cities, giant roots, abandoned Ember mines, flooded ruins, machinery, collapsed sanctums, and enormous caverns carry themes of **memory, loss, and buried truth**. Mechanical posture is **adapt**: familiar terrain and threats can occupy alternate Ember states. The major revelation is CANON direction: ancient authorities deliberately centralized Ember, network activation substantially contributed to catastrophe, and continued restraint drives decline.

Visual states must differ by silhouette, pattern, motion, and material response—not only hue. Waterlines, root growth, machinery rhythm, inscriptions, and spatial scars tell history without terminal dumps.

## Ember Polarity

Polarity toggles authored structures between compatible states: platforms, hazards, currents, doors, and defenses. It does not duplicate every object or create a separate movement engine. Introduction occurs in safe, local pairs; later switches change broader rooms with previewed consequences. State changes must never materialize collision through Nova. Bounce pads, checkpoints, moving platforms, Pulse, and currents retain defined behavior in both states.

## Structure and briefs

1. **3-1 Descent of Roots:** safe local polarity introduction during a downward journey; old World 1 platform language anchors the player. Secrets show roots thriving where conduits were bypassed.
2. **3-2 The Drowned Archive:** water/ruin navigation and polarity-dependent records. Alternating platforms expose official versus suppressed archive layers.
3. **Midpoint — The Keeper Below:** a shorter encounter testing state observation rather than boss-scale endurance. Its defeat/withdrawal opens the city but does not complete the world. Narrative role: first explicit accusation that containment was chosen.
4. **3-3 City Beneath Cinders:** broad urban traversal where residents' absence and infrastructure tell consequences. Combine currents, polarity, doors, and defensive enemies; preserve clear route anchors.
5. **3-4 The First Reservoir:** approach and synthesis. Trace the network's earliest large containment site, with optional testimony showing alternatives were known and rejected.
6. **Boss — The Hollow Regent:** custodian of stored Ember and controlled history. Arena alternates offensive/defensive geometry with advance telegraphs and safe transition handling. The Regent challenges Nova's right to risk another catastrophe.

## Enemy roles (PROPOSED)

- **Phase Burrower:** occupies one polarity state at a time, teaching observation and timing.
- **Dredge Bell:** slow area-denial pulse that changes which surfaces are dangerous.
- **Rootbound Watcher:** defensive anchor whose protection shifts between linked enemies/objects.

Adapt existing Turrets, Armored enemies, Jumpers, and Flyers before adding more. Target roughly three new roles.

## Secret-history progression

Approximately 10–14 secrets. Normal progression proves deliberate network activation. Secrets reveal who objected, how records were edited, and which regional systems survived. A Veil route connects living root growth to off-network Ember; completionists can identify the First Keeper's role before the Regent confirms the network's structure.

## NPC use

Talen should confront curated history in the Drowned Archive. Mira may appear after the midpoint to keep revelation from becoming despair. Vesper belongs only where a genuine Veil route intersects; Orin's perspective may arrive through later dialogue rather than physical presence. Exact appearances are PROPOSED.

## Production cautions

Do not make polarity a constant toggle tax. Rooms should present intention, preview, action, and consequence. Prototype state safety, checkpoint reconstruction, projectiles, current interaction, editor preview, validation, save implications, and effects-off readability before authoring full levels.
