World 1 behavior below is CANON. Later appearances are PROPOSED and should occur only when a character changes a decision, interpretation, or emotional rhythm.

|---|---|---|---|---|

## Relationship with Nova

Nova earns trust through attention and action, not errands. NPCs may disagree sharply; none is a morality score, quest dispenser, shopkeeper, or mandatory approval gate. Dialogue should react to existing progression/secrets and remain concise. A recurring character should have changed knowledge, stakes, or position since the prior appearance.

Recommended distribution is 2–3 meaningful appearances per later world across the whole cast, not per character. Do not place every NPC in every world or level.
