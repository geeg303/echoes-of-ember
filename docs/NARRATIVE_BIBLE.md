# Narrative Bible

## Timeline: actual historical truth

1. **The Living Ember — CANON:** Ember flows naturally through regions, carrying creation, memory, transformation, and renewal. Communities develop different relationships with it.
2. **Harnessing — CANON:** civilizations build conduits, observatories, reservoirs, and sanctums. Benefits grow alongside genuine accidents and instability.
3. **The Safety Mandate — CANON:** guardians create Wardens and a network to regulate dangerous surges. The original motive is understandable protection.
4. **Centralization — CANON:** authority and flow consolidate. Regional practice is treated as risk; protecting people becomes protecting the network's control.
5. **The remembered catastrophe — CANON direction:** activation/escalation of containment contributes substantially to collapse. Public memory reduces this to uncontrolled Ember disaster.
6. **Long restraint — CANON:** divided, contained Ember can no longer renew the world adequately. Decline is gradual enough that the old explanation persists.
7. **Nova's journey — CANON:** scattered light, living exceptions, records, Wardens, and Veil routes allow the truth to be reconstructed.

Exact dates, ancient polity names, and whether the First Keeper began as a person or intelligence are **OPEN QUESTIONS**.

## Knowledge progression

| World | Player/Nova knowledge on the normal path | Actual truth available through secrets |
|---|---|---|
| Verdant Reaches | Ember has faded; ruins and a hostile guardian block restoration. | Ember Veil proves Ember persists outside official Warden routes. |
| Glassreach Expanse | Official observatory records conflict; the Sentinel is obeying intact instructions. | Measurements and hidden routes show containment redirected flow before the supposed disaster. |
| Hollow Deep | The network was activated deliberately and helped cause collapse; restraint causes present decline. | Suppressed testimony identifies regional alternatives erased from the archive and points toward the First Keeper. |
| Crown of Cinders | Restoring the central network would reproduce the problem; control and restoration differ. | Full Veil history demonstrates decentralized stewardship is possible but demanding. |

Normal-path players must understand every decision needed for the ending. Secret-path players gain earlier evidence, names, nuance, and emotional context—not an exclusive coherent plot.

## Nova's arc

- **Discovery:** hopeful repairer; follows the world's visible guidance.
- **Doubt:** listens to contradictions rather than defending her first story.
- **Revelation:** accepts that well-intended institutions can create catastrophe without a simple villain.
- **Choice:** acts under uncertainty, rejects both total release and frozen control, and accepts responsibility for gradual change.

## NPC arcs

- **Mira — curiosity/hope:** validates exploration, then learns hope requires revising cherished stories. Proposed appearances: World 2 opening or aftermath; World 4 convergence.
- **Orin — survival/safety:** challenges reckless idealism and gives the Keeper's strongest practical argument a human voice. Proposed appearances: World 2 hazard refuge; World 4 before commitment.
- **Talen — history/evidence:** moves from reading official ruins to admitting the archive was curated. Proposed appearances: Observatory and Hollow archive; supplies evidence, not answers.
- **Vesper — Veil/alternative history:** knows hidden routes are real but should not become an omniscient expositor. Proposed appearances: secret-linked spaces across worlds and final optional context.

They may disagree with Nova and each other. None becomes a quest dispenser, currency vendor, or progression authority by default.

## Boss narrative roles

- **Ashen Warden — CANON:** first encounter with institutional guardianship; teaches boss language and suggests protection can harden into confinement.
- **Luminous Sentinel — PROPOSED:** functioning observatory guardian. Its correctness under ancient instructions makes the system—not corruption—the question.
- **Hollow Regent — PROPOSED:** custodian of buried memory and reservoirs; defends suppression as necessary continuity.
- **First Keeper — PROPOSED identity:** central architect/authority sincerely convinced instability causes unacceptable suffering. It presents the best case for control.

## Ember Veil throughline

The shipped Ember Veil map branch is **CANON evidence**, not merely a bonus room. Later secret exits reveal a chain of communities, maintenance routes, testimony, and local Ember practices outside official channels. Secret Tokens may authenticate/access deeper optional Veil records (**PROPOSED**) but never purchase power or gate the normal ending.

## Revelation pacing

Environment establishes consequences; NPCs interpret them imperfectly; bosses embody competing policies; ordinary objectives uncover enough facts to proceed; secrets reveal suppressed alternatives. Never deliver the entire truth in one monologue, terminal, or villain confession. Each world should pose a question before answering part of the previous one.

## Finale and ending

Stage I confronts the Keeper's embodied defense. Stage II traverses the network using currents, polarity, combat, and World 1 movement vocabulary. Stage III reaches the Ember source while the Keeper continues resistance. Nova redirects control outward in a gradual process. Regions regain agency; Wardens lose central command; danger and work remain. Final image/theme: the world can change again.
