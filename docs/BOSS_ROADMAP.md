# Full-Game Boss Roadmap

All bosses follow readable anticipation → active threat → recovery, use world mechanics, preserve damage/result authority, and communicate vulnerability without color/audio alone. Escalation comes from decisions and meaning, not health inflation.

## The Ashen Warden — CANON implemented

- **Purpose:** first institutional guardian; introduces protection becoming confinement.
- **Lesson:** read telegraphs, dodge, punish exposed core with Ember Pulse.
- **Mechanics:** 18 health, 1 contact/projectile damage, non-stompable, encounter-safe Pulse, arena doors, checkpoint.
- **Phases:** Ground Slam/Bolt/Heavy Advance; Slam/Double Bolt/Rain/Leap; Fast Slam/Rain/Charge-Leap/Core Burst.
- **Arena/tone:** compact First Flame Sanctum, awe and awakening; clear core vulnerability and three-second defeat.

This summary is subordinate to the shipped configuration and `ASHEN_WARDEN.md`.

## The Luminous Sentinel — PROPOSED

- **Purpose:** show a Warden functioning correctly under harmful instructions.
- **Lesson:** environment can expose truth and vulnerability; use current direction intentionally.
- **Mechanics:** redirect currents/projectiles, moving prismatic apertures, positional safe lanes.
- **Phases:** learn current-assisted avoidance; manipulate flow to open core; combine intersecting flow with familiar attacks.
- **Arena/tone:** observatory/vault, precise and luminous; emotional result is doubt, not triumph over corruption.

## The Keeper Below — PROPOSED midpoint encounter

- **Purpose:** voice/embody the first direct contradiction before World 3's full reveal.
- **Lesson:** observe polarity state and adapt rather than maximizing damage.
- **Mechanics:** short state-linked hazards/defenses; boss-like lifecycle without world-completion authority.
- **Arena/tone:** drowned archival chamber; tense, incomplete, possibly withdrawal rather than death.

## The Hollow Regent — PROPOSED

- **Purpose:** defend buried memory and containment as continuity; reveal deliberate activation.
- **Lesson:** plan safe state changes while under pressure.
- **Mechanics:** polarity-linked armor, reservoir geometry, currents, familiar projectiles in altered states.
- **Phases:** establish state vulnerability; invert safe/threat geometry; combine state timing with environmental control.
- **Arena/tone:** monumental reservoir, grief and accusation. Defeat reveals systemic responsibility.

## The First Keeper — PROPOSED final antagonist

- **Purpose:** make the strongest principled case for centralized safety and force Nova's final choice.
- **Lesson:** mastery is not mere execution; use the whole game's language to redistribute authority.
- **Mechanics:** Warden attacks, currents, polarity, traversal, interactables, Pulse, and power-up choices.
- **Stages:** Keeper combat → Network traversal/combat → Ember source intervention.
- **Arena/tone:** controlled grandeur giving way to unstable possibility; ending is gradual decentralization.

Exact later boss names, health, attacks, damage, and rewards remain open until their world prototypes establish pacing.
