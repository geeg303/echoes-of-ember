# World 2 Design — The Glassreach Expanse

**Status:** PROPOSED working name and production brief. No level data is authorized by this document.

## Identity

Crystalline plains, wind-carved mesas, suspended ruins, reflective caverns, broken observatories, and exposed Ember conduits create a bright world whose apparent clarity is deceptive. Emotional theme: **certainty versus truth**. Narrative question: **Who told us what happened?** Mechanical identity: momentum plus environmental interaction through Ember Currents.

Visual language uses long sightlines, diagonal wind ribbons, prismatic silhouettes, mirrored surfaces, and ancient measurement marks. Current direction must remain readable through moving motes/streamlines, arrows or geometry, and sound—not color alone. Stable terrain stays visually distinct from crystal decoration.

## Narrative purpose

Nova arrives expecting evidence of a natural Ember failure. Observatory records disagree with each other and with the landscape. Talen can help distinguish measurement from interpretation; Orin can articulate why communities accepted a safer official account. The Luminous Sentinel is not malfunctioning: it applies valid ancient directives, proving correct operation can still serve a harmful system. Secret routes expose redirected flow and off-network survival earlier.

## New mechanic: Ember Currents

Teach horizontal push over safe ground; test lift and landing; combine with moving platforms and Wind Boots; redirect Ember Pulse through a controlled puzzle; let selected enemies exploit/resist flow; culminate in Sentinel arena routing. Currents must never make a mandatory route permanently fail after a timed power expires. See `MECHANICAL_PROGRESSION.md` for required runtime/editor/validator work.

## Level briefs

1. **2-1 Shardwind Crossing:** welcoming crystalline plain. Teach visible horizontal currents, safe momentum, Crawler/Flyer reuse, and a low-risk upper current route. Secret: an old route marker points against the official flow.
2. **2-2 The Mirrored Caves:** reflection and vertical lift. Test current entry/exit timing, Aether Wing alternatives, breakable sightline puzzles, and an enemy whose silhouette reveals the real body. Secrets use imperfect reflections and airflow clues, never arbitrary invisible walls.
3. **2-3 Observatory of Echoes:** projectile redirection and evidence. Currents rotate/redirect Ember Pulse into switches while normal cover remains. Talen is the recommended recurring NPC. Records first contradict the catastrophe account without explaining the full conspiracy.
4. **2-4 The Broken Meridian:** strongest traversal combination. Suspended ruins, moving platforms, changing current lanes, limited turrets, and recoverable falls. Orin may appear at a refuge to challenge Nova's growing certainty.
5. **2-5 Prismheart Vault:** pre-boss synthesis. Current-controlled doors/platforms, armored pressure, optional mastery routes, and a calm approach to the Vault. Establish that the Sentinel's orders are intact.

## Enemy roles (PROPOSED)

- **Sailback:** current-riding area denial; predictable lane sweeps, vulnerable when flow reverses.
- **Prism Mimic:** reflection/target-identification challenge, not a simple teleport pursuer.
- **Conduit Keeper:** anchors or redirects a local current until interrupted; environmental priority target.
- **Glass Skater:** momentum-based ground threat that commits to readable paths and rewards repositioning.

Reuse Crawlers, Flyers, Turrets, Jumpers, and Armored enemies where their existing role supports the lesson. Target only 3–4 new families.

## Secrets and NPCs

Approximately 12–16 secrets across five levels. Each level should contain a current-opposed route or observation inconsistency; deeper routes reveal measurements hidden from official records. Secret Tokens may unlock optional Veil testimony after World 2, not required power. Recommended NPC use: Talen in Observatory, Orin once at a safe refuge, Vesper only on a secret route if it adds rather than explains mystery; Mira may frame departure/aftermath, not follow every stage.

## Boss: Luminous Sentinel

Arena is a prismatic observatory with stable safe zones and controllable current lanes. Phase concepts: learn current-assisted dodging; redirect Pulse or environment to expose the core; then combine moving apertures and multi-direction flow. The emotional tone is awe and unease, not rage. Its defeat yields the realization that obedience—not corruption—caused the conflict. Exact attacks/health remain OPEN QUESTIONS for Milestone D.

## Production gates

Milestone B prototypes one current field, projectile redirection, editor handles, validation, debug overlay, accessibility-off readability, and one enemy interaction. Only after feel/performance/softlock review should Milestone C author five levels.
