# Secret and Ember Veil Roadmap

## Canon foundation

Verdant Reaches ships 12 same-level secrets: 2 in Verdant Beginning, 3 in Whispering Canopy, 3 in Emberfall Ravine, and 4 in Ruins. They include caches, rooms, challenges, alternate routes, and one secret exit. Each normal level has one Secret Token. `v04_secret_exit` reveals the Ember Veil map node and persists monotonically.

## Full-game purpose

Secrets reward curiosity mechanically and narratively. The Ember Veil is evidence that Ember survives beyond official Warden infrastructure. Later secret exits form a loose network of hidden routes, communities, testimony, and regional practices. Completionists learn major truths earlier and with greater nuance; the normal story never becomes incoherent without them.

## Fair hiding rules

- Establish clue language before asking for inference: cracks, rising shard trails, altered vines/crystals, airflow, ruin symbols, reflections, state mismatch.
- Use at least two signals for deep secrets where practical; never depend only on invisible collision.
- A visible reward may teach destination, but entry remains discoverable.
- Optional power-ups can ease or enrich a route, never create expiration softlocks.
- Secret paths reconnect or provide explicit safe return.
- Effects Off, muted audio, and color-vision differences retain clues.
- Challenge rooms name stable target sets and award once.

## World revelations (PROPOSED)

- **World 1:** Ember can persist outside the Warden network.
- **World 2:** hidden measurements show the network redirected flow before the public catastrophe account.
- **World 3:** testimony and infrastructure prove alternatives were suppressed and containment was deliberate.
- **World 4:** living Veil routes demonstrate decentralized stewardship is possible, difficult, and already practiced.

Secret Tokens remain deep-exploration markers. Recommended use is optional lore/Veil access thresholds across worlds—not shops, ordinary currency, or mandatory ending gates. Exact thresholds are OPEN QUESTIONS.

Approximate targets: World 2 12–16 secrets, World 3 10–14, World 4 12–16. Counts remain flexible until levels exist.
