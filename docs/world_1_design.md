# Verdant Reaches — World 1 design

Registry order is Verdant Beginning → Whispering Canopy → Emberfall Ravine → Ruins of the First Flame. Difficulty rises from introductory to a fair World-1 culmination while visuals progress from open grove, to high canopy, glowing ravine, and nature-bound ruins.

| Level | Purpose | Sections | Collectibles | Enemy emphasis | Power-up emphasis |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Verdant Beginning | Teach the foundation | 8 | 52 shards, 3 rare, 1 token | Gradual archetype introductions | Ember Pulse lesson |
| Whispering Canopy | Vertical and aerial control | 8 | 56 shards, 3 rare, 1 token | Flyers with light ground threats | Aether Wing featured |
| Emberfall Ravine | Momentum and unstable terrain | 8 | 62 shards, 3 rare, 1 token | Turrets and Armored enemies | Wind Boots and Ember Pulse |
| Ruins of the First Flame | Readable combinations | 8 | 68 shards, 4 rare, 1 token | Balanced five-archetype mix | Controlled choices |

## Reusable patterns

- Crawler plus low platforms teaches spacing.
- Flyer plus vertical climbing controls airspace safely.
- Turret plus cover permits timing or Ember Pulse solutions.
- Falling platforms first appear above recoverable routes.
- Moving platforms carry collectible timing arcs.
- Armored enemies sit near Ember Pulse opportunities without blocking progress.
- Switches use visible doors and validated stable IDs.
- Featured power-ups receive a safe demonstration before combination.

Checkpoints follow meaningful milestones. Optional upper, breakable, and power-assisted routes reconnect to the mandatory path. Timed effects improve safety, speed, combat, or collection but never become permanent progression gates.
