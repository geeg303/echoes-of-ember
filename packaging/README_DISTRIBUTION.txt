ECHOES OF EMBER — WORLD 1 VERTICAL SLICE
Version 0.1.0-alpha

This pre-alpha player build contains the complete Verdant Reaches vertical slice.

CONTROLS
Move: A/D or arrows     Jump: Space
Attack: F               Interact: E
Pause: Escape           Menus: arrows, Enter, Escape
Controllers use the left stick/D-pad, face buttons, and Start. Prompts adapt automatically.

Use Settings for volume, fullscreen, vibration, and Full/Reduced/Off effects. Progress, settings,
and achievements are stored in your operating system's per-user application-data directory, not
inside this game folder. You can delete the extracted game folder without deleting your saves.

Worlds 2–4 are not included. Procedural placeholder presentation remains. Wider physical-controller,
audio, fullscreen, antivirus, and distribution testing is still recommended.
